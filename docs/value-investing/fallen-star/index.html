CANDIDATE FILTER · CATEGORY 01

Fallen Star

Find stocks that have fallen sharply and may be approaching the start of a rebound. Choose a future month, then narrow the timing matches to companies that were growing before the drop.

THE IDEA

What counts as a Fallen Star?

A big drop begins when a stock's adjusted closing price falls at least 20% below its preceding 12-month high. We estimate the month its price may begin to bounce. A predicted month is an estimate, not a confirmed rebound.

For historical testing, a bounce onset is a rise of at least 15% from the post-drop low that holds into the following month. The health check looks at growth reported before the drop; it does not decide whether anything fundamental changed afterward.

HYPOTHETICAL EXAMPLE

  1. September 2026Stock A falls 22% from its prior high.
  2. November 2026Predicted bounce onset: two elapsed months after the drop.
  3. December 2026Earliest confirmation under the proposed historical rule.

Selecting November would show Stock A. A stock predicted to start bouncing in July 2027 would appear under July 2027 instead.
